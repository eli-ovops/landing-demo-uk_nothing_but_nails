## Full-page visual-storyboard consequence

There is **no approved customer visual corpus** in this package. A future Demo cannot honestly satisfy the client-specific full-page visual storyboard by filling the page with generic nail imagery or by treating platform images as the salon’s portfolio. Before any Demo work, the Owner must either:

1. re-open and confirm a customer-controlled official social or owned website, then capture only eligible assets with original files, hashes, source URLs, placement and rights records; or
2. decide that the available visual evidence is insufficient for a client-showable Demo and return to research/hold.

Original concept images are not a substitute for client identity, real work, people, salon, services or results. No concept-image generation has been requested or performed.

## Evidence-backed purchase thesis (research hypothesis only)

**Current Offer:** United Kingdom / GBP / `£99 AS-SHOWN HANDOVER` or `£199 ONE PERSONALISED UPDATE`.

The current identifiable presence divides the same salon across a booking platform, a landlord/location page and a candidate social link. A static single page could give a visitor one independently shareable first read of the Arundel salon, its verified core nail-service categories and a clear onward route to existing Fresha booking — without recreating booking, payment or customer-data systems.

### Five-second before / after commercial contrast

- **Before:** a visitor encounters a booking profile or a location listing; identity, service overview, local context and booking route are split across surfaces.
- **After (hypothesis):** one clearly Arundel-focused, factual static introduction makes the salon identity and core service choice legible before handing the visitor to existing booking.

This is not a claim of conversion, growth, bookings, revenue, purchase intent or a need for a more attractive design.
